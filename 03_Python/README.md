# Business Insights

## 1. Overall Business Performance
- Total sales reached approximately $2.30 million.
- Total profit was approximately $286,397.
- The overall profit margin was approximately 12.47%.
- The business generated 5,009 unique orders.
- Average sales per transaction were approximately $229.86.

The business generated substantial revenue and maintained a positive profit margin. However, further analysis of product categories, discounts, and regional performance can help identify opportunities to improve profitability.

## 2. Category Analysis
- Technology generated the highest sales and profit among the three categories.
- Office Supplies generated the second-highest profit despite having lower sales than Furniture.
- Furniture generated considerable sales but comparatively low profit.

Furniture profitability should be investigated by reviewing product costs, pricing strategies, and discount levels.

## 3. Sub-Category Analysis
- Phones generated the highest sales among sub-categories.
- Chairs were also among the highest-selling sub-categories.
- Copiers generated the highest profit despite not having the highest sales.
- Phones, Accessories, and Paper also contributed significantly to profit.

The business should prioritize high-profit sub-categories while reviewing the profitability of high-sales products that generate comparatively lower profits.

## 4. Regional Analysis
- The West region generated the highest sales and profit.
- The East region was the second-best-performing region.
- Central generated the lowest profit despite having higher sales than South.
- South generated lower sales but higher profit than Central.

The Central region requires further investigation into product mix, discounting, and profitability. The West and East regions could provide opportunities for further growth.

## 5. Customer Segment Analysis
- Consumer customers contributed the largest share of sales and profit.
- Corporate customers generated the second-highest sales and profit.
- Home Office customers contributed the lowest sales and profit.

The business should focus on retaining Consumer customers while exploring opportunities to increase sales from Corporate customers.

## 6. Customer Analysis
- Sean Miller was the highest-selling customer.
- Tamara Chand and Raymond Buch were among the other top customers.
- A relatively small group of customers contributed significant sales.

Maintaining relationships with high-value customers through customer retention strategies and targeted offers may help support revenue.

## 7. Product Analysis
- Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier was the highest-selling product.
- It also generated the highest profit among the analyzed products.
- Several other technology products performed strongly in both sales and profit.

The business should identify its most profitable products and prioritize inventory and marketing resources accordingly.

## 8. Loss-Making Products
- Cubify CubeX 3D Printer Double Head generated the highest product-level loss.
- Lexmark MX611dhe Monochrome Laser Printer also generated substantial losses.
- Other loss-making products included Cubify CubeX 3D Printer Triple Head and certain conference tables.

The business should review the pricing, costs, discounts, and demand of loss-making products. Some products may require pricing adjustments or changes to promotional strategies.

## 9. Discount Analysis
- Transactions without discounts generated the highest total profit.
- Discounts of 30% and above generally resulted in negative total profits.
- Higher discount levels were associated with greater losses.
- Discounts of 70% and 80% resulted in particularly poor profitability.

The business should review its discount policies and consider establishing discount limits to protect profit margins.

## 10. Yearly Sales and Profit Trends
- Sales declined slightly in 2015 compared with 2014.
- Sales increased significantly from 2015 onward.
- Profit increased every year from 2014 to 2017.
- 2017 was the strongest year for both sales and profit.

The business demonstrated improving performance in the later years. Further analysis could identify the products, regions, and customer segments responsible for this growth.

## 11. Correlation Analysis
- Sales and Profit showed a moderate positive correlation.
- Discount and Profit showed a negative correlation.
- Quantity had a weak relationship with Profit.
- Sales and Quantity showed a weak positive relationship.

The correlation analysis suggests that higher sales are generally associated with higher profit, while higher discounts are associated with lower profit. However, correlation does not prove causation.

# Business Recommendations

- Focus on high-profit products such as Copiers, Phones, and Accessories.
- Investigate the profitability of Furniture products.
- Review high-discount transactions to reduce losses.
- Prioritize strong-performing regions such as West and East.
- Investigate the lower profitability of the Central region.
- Develop customer retention strategies for high-value customers.
- Analyze the business growth experienced during 2016 and 2017.
- Review loss-making products and consider pricing or promotional adjustments.
